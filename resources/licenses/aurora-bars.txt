Aurora Bars
Reference: https://ui.unlumen.com/components/aurora-bars
Author/site: unlumen UI
Original concept credited by the reference: @SannaGranqvistX

The home background adapts the publicly displayed aurora-bars.tsx height
calculation, gradient, bar geometry and vignette to vanilla JavaScript/CSS.
It uses 16 touching bars to match the public demo, height bounds 0.18/0.92,
opacity 0.85 and the pink gradient. Local tuning adds 2px blur, speed 1.5,
and smoothly randomized phases/frequencies for independent height drift.

The React/Motion dependency is not required by this Laravel implementation.
Visibility and reduced-motion controls were added locally.
